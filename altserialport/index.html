About

AltSerialPort is a terminal for serial ports, made for low-level debugging of hardware: microcontroller boards, USB-UART adapters, modems, GPS and industrial devices. Choose a port, press Connect, read and type. When the board resets or the cable is pulled, it waits and opens the port again. The data is shown as text, hex or both, with timestamps down to microseconds, and everything else is one right-click away: logs, live plots, macros, triggers, file transfer and a second port. AI agents can work with a real board through its MCP server. English and Russian, light and dark themes.

What it does

Finds your device

Every port with its chip, like COM5 · CP2102. A device you plug in appears in the list by itself.

Survives resets

When the board resets, is re-flashed or its cable is pulled, the port opens again as soon as it is back.

Text, hex or both

Text with ANSI colors, hex bytes split into packets, or text with every control byte shown as \r, \n, \x00.

Sends what you need

Keys go to the device as you type. Commands have history, hex bytes, escapes and checksums such as CRC-16 Modbus.

Plots numbers

Numbers in the received lines, like t=23.4 h=41, become a live chart with no setup.

Macros and triggers

Commands on Shift+F1…F12. A text or a pattern can color lines, beep or answer the device.

Two ports and a bridge

Both sides of a link on one time line, or data passed between two ports or to TCP.

Logs and file transfer

Records every connection to a file. Sends and receives files by XMODEM and YMODEM.

Works with AI agents

altserialport mcp lets Claude or another agent open a port, write, read and reset a board.
