Install

  1. Open the .dmg and drag AltHex to Applications.
  2. Start it from Applications or Launchpad. The app is signed and notarized, so it opens without the Gatekeeper warning.

Builds are for Apple Silicon (M1 and newer) only. Intel Macs are not supported.
